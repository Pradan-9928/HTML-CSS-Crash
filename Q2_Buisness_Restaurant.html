  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (or 350°F if using a brown butter or softer recipe)
  2. Combine dry ingredients by stirring the flour, baking soda, and salt together in a small bowl
  3. Mix the wet ingredients by beating the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar in a large bowl until cream
  4. Combine: Gradually stir the flour mixture into the wet ingredients, then fold in 2 cups of chocolate chips.
  5. Bake: Scoop tablespoon-sized balls onto a baking sheet and bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are golden.
